Over the summer of 2026, I designed a H Bridge Motor Driver PCB for a solar emulater for my design team. The solar emulator would test for power generated by the solar panels which would be used for the CubeSat. 

This PCB features a compact, high-current motor control board for driving DC motors under heavy dynamic loads. It integrates the minimal design of an ESP32 microcontroller with a motor driver circuit containing a motor driver with an H-Bridge topology. This design allows for wireless telemetry, closed-loop motor control and real time, wireless fault monitoring.

## Specification:
* ESP32-S3-WROOM module with dual-core processing power, onboard WIFI, and BLE for remote telemetry and wireless communication.
* Utilized a dedicated, high-voltage HIP4081AIPZ full bridge driver coupled with low, N-channel IRFZ44N MOSFETs to handle efficient current stalling.
* Implemented a precise, current-sensing resistors and  op-amp (LM393) feedback circuit to monitor motor load and detect stall conditions.
* Features a multi-rail power distribution network with robust input filtering and LDO regulators to motor logic from controller logic.

## Challenges:
* Designing a compact PCB was a challenge when using THT components. The tradeoff of using THT components is a more mechanically stronger design at the cost of routing and space. Switching over to SMD components made laying out components easy which efficitevly helped with routing and a compact design.
* Handled high current densities by increasing track width, copper pours and strategic thermal via placement to help mitigate overheating.
* Minimized high-current loop areas around the H-birdge and strategicially separated analog feedback lines from digital switching traces to maintain signal integrity and supress EMI.
